👥 About EEPY

EEPY was created by six University of Melbourne students as part of Atlassian x UNIHACK (UNIHACK 2025). Our goal? To tackle the silent but deadly danger of drowsy driving using AI-powered real-time monitoring.

➡️ Try EEPY Now

⚠️ Why Drowsy Driving is a Serious Problem

  • Drowsy driving isn’t just about falling asleep at the wheel—even a brief lapse in concentration can have serious consequences.
  • In Victoria, fatigue is a factor in 16-20% of all road crashes—a statistic too significant to ignore.
  • Unlike speeding or drunk driving, fatigue-related crashes are harder to detect and prevent—until now.

    • 🎯 Who is EEPY for?

    EEPY is designed for:

    ✅ Long-distance drivers who travel for extended periods.
    ✅ Shift workers who drive home late at night.
    ✅ Rideshare & delivery drivers who spend long hours on the road.
    ✅ Everyday commuters who risk drowsiness during routine drives.


    EEPY helps anyone who drives stay alert and safe on the road.

    🚀 How EEPY Works

    EEPY is a real-time AI system that:

    🟣 Monitors your face for signs of drowsiness using advanced facial tracking.
    🟡 Detects drowsy behaviour like excessive blinking & yawning.
    🟢 Sends real-time alerts & engages in AI-driven conversation to keep you awake.


    💡 EEPY doesn’t just tell you you’re sleepy—it actively helps keep you alert.

    🔍 Understanding EAR & MAR (Displayed on Camera Page)

    EEPY uses two key indicators to detect drowsiness in drivers:

    1️⃣ EAR (Eye Aspect Ratio) – Measures how open or closed your eyes are. If your eyes start to close frequently, it’s a sign of drowsiness.
    2️⃣ MAR (Mouth Aspect Ratio) – Tracks mouth movement to detect yawning. Frequent yawning is another strong indicator of fatigue.


    EEPY continuously analyses both EAR & MAR in real time to determine if you need an alert.
